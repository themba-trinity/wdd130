The Rapids

Our goal is to give you an unforgettable memory navigating the river with our highly qualified guides. White water rafting with us is a once-in-a-lifetime experience.

History

The rapids were formed thousands of years ago through geological processes, attracting outdoor adventurers ever since. What began as a frontier game has grown into a celebrated destination for rafting.

Today we operate daily with a team of qualified guides, adventure is our game. weather you are a first time rafter or seasoned adventurer, our goal is to create a memorable experience and we believe in fostering a spirit of community and respect nature.
